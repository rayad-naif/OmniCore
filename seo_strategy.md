# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing site pages in `artifacts/marketing-site/`, especially `/`, `/pricing`, `/contact`, `/help`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, and `/refunds`
- Static crawlable assets that affect discovery and sharing for the marketing site (`robots.txt`, sitemap, OG assets, favicon, static legal/pricing HTML pages)
- AI/social crawler visibility for the marketing site’s public routes

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard routes under `/dashboard/**` in `artifacts/dashboard/`
- Internal API endpoints under `/api/**` and operational widget assets unless they directly affect search crawler behavior for public marketing pages
- Demo or transactional utility surfaces not intended as search landing pages (for example widget demo endpoints and checkout-success flows), except where their indexation behavior is caused by shared marketing-site routing/head configuration

## Target audience
- Businesses evaluating omnichannel helpdesk and AI customer support software
- Teams comparing pricing, support workflow tooling, and AI-assisted support platforms

## Primary keywords
- omnichannel helpdesk software
- AI customer support platform
- multi-tenant helpdesk SaaS
- customer support dashboard
- helpdesk pricing
